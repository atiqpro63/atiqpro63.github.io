## 🐾 What Is Pawboard?

Most note-taking apps ask you to leave what you're doing. You open a window, find the app, wait for it to load, take your note, and then close it again — hoping you'll remember to come back later.

Pawboard flips that idea. It places a full whiteboard directly on your Windows desktop wallpaper layer, sitting behind your icons and shortcuts. When you're not using it, it's invisible enough to ignore. When you need it, it's already there — one glance away.

Think of it as the paper pad you keep next to your keyboard, except it never gets lost, never runs out of pages, and can be as big as your entire screen.

Whether you're someone who likes to keep a running to-do list visible all day, someone who sketches ideas while on calls, or someone who just wants their desktop to feel a little more personal — Pawboard is built for exactly that.

## 🖱️ Your First Five Minutes

New to desktop whiteboards? Here's a gentle way to start.

**Minute 1 — Get the lay of the land.** Look at your desktop. The area behind your icons is now your board. Nothing here can break, so feel free to experiment.

**Minute 2 — Write something short.** Jot down one thing you want to remember today. A single word or a short phrase is plenty. Notice how it stays visible even while you work in other apps.

**Minute 3 — Draw a shape.** Draw a rough box, a circle, or an arrow. Try connecting two notes together. This is the fastest way to see how Pawboard differs from a plain text file.

**Minute 4 — Arrange your space.** Move your note somewhere on the board where it won't be hidden by your icons. Corners and empty regions usually work best.

**Minute 5 — Leave it alone.** Close everything, use your computer normally, and come back later. Your board will still be there.

That's the whole learning curve. Everything else is refinement.

## 🎨 Personalizing Your Board

Pawboard is built around the idea that your desktop should feel like yours. That extends to the whiteboard itself.

Adjust the background appearance so your board reads clearly against your wallpaper. If you use a bright, busy wallpaper, a softer board surface keeps your notes legible. If your desktop is dark and minimal, you can push the board toward a lighter or more contrasty look.

The goal is simple: your notes should be easy to read at a glance, without you having to squint or hunt for them. A minute of tweaking at the start saves a lot of squinting later.

## 🧠 Tips for Getting the Most Out of Pawboard

**Keep it sparse.** The board is most useful when it holds a few things you actually care about. If it fills up, clear it out — a fresh board is a fresh start.

**Use zones.** Mentally divide your desktop into regions: top-left for tasks, bottom-right for sketches, center for the thing you're working on right now. Zones make it easy to find things without searching.

**Avoid the icon-heavy areas.** Your icons live in predictable places. Place notes where they won't be covered, especially if you keep a lot of shortcuts on your desktop.

**Make it part of your routine.** Check your board at the start of the day and clear it at the end. Two seconds, twice a day, and it stays genuinely useful.

**Don't over-plan.** The best boards evolve naturally. Start with one note and let it grow into whatever you need.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**The board doesn't appear after launching.**
Click once on an empty area of your desktop to refresh it. If other windows are open, minimize them to see the desktop layer properly.

**My notes are hard to read against my wallpaper.**
Adjust the board's background appearance so there's enough contrast between the surface and your writing.

**The download didn't start.**
Return to the project page and try again from there. Make sure your browser isn't blocking the download.

**Windows shows a warning when I open the file.**
This is a standard Windows safety prompt for files downloaded from the internet. Confirm that you want to run the file to continue.

**My board looks different after a Windows update.**
Desktop-layer tools can occasionally be affected by major Windows updates. If something looks off, restart Pawboard and check the project page for the latest version.

**I want to start completely fresh.**
Clear the board and begin again. A blank canvas is always one action away.
